## How engagement works

1. **Discover** — clarify users, business needs, constraints and ownership.
2. **Define** — scope functionality, data, integrations, delivery routes and acceptance tests.
3. **Design** — establish the experience, content hierarchy and technical architecture.
4. **Develop** — implement, integrate, test, deploy and document.
5. **Evolve** — measure, support, train and improve.

This is the five-stage public services model. The profile's four-card 'Discover / Design / Engineer / Evolve' graphic is a **visual simplification**, not a replacement for the full five-step process.

## Typical entry points

- New build: start with objectives, design, implementation and launch.
- Existing digital estate: audit, improve high-friction points, optimize or modernize.
- Connected operation: integrate tools, automate defined tasks and improve reporting.
- Ongoing service: maintenance, content operations, training or support.
